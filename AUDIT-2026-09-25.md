# What a review of the five unreviewed commits found, 2026-09-25

## What this is

The magnetism and ultracell review of 2026-09-23 (`PLAN.md` P109) was the last independent
reading of the package, and it found fifteen defects. P110 and P111 were written with a
reviewer beside every fixer, so what had been read by nobody but its author is the five
single-session commits after them, about 600 lines of package code:

| commit | what it changed | record |
|---|---|---|
| `1fba737`, `693529d` | the Anderson fit in `rho_ddot`'s inner product, off by default | P113 |
| `a9eb343` | one spin trace for all four `fcoef` sandwiches at `soc_scale = 0` | P115 |
| `bde3bed` | three Part III edits landed bit-identical | P112 |
| `7efbc3d` | the gradient correction gated on `|rho|`, a negative point's energy signed | P116 |

Four reviewers, one per change, each walking the `trap-check` list and the vendored
Fortran, and behind each a skeptic told to refute every finding by reading the code again
rather than the reviewer's quotes. 8 agents, 0 errors, 30 minutes, 321 tool calls. No agent
ran anything. **12 findings: 11 confirmed by the skeptic, 1 plausible, none refuted**, and
the skeptic raised one of them from medium to high. One finding was then measured in the
main session (the first section below). Everything else is a reading of the source and
carries no number, so none of it belongs in `PLAN.md` until it has one.

## Measured: `soc_scale = 0` leaves PAW's small-component magnetization on **[closed 2026-09-25, `PLAN.md` P117]**

Closed the same day by scaling the small-component tensor with `soc_scale`, the choice
of three put to the user (scale it, keep it and document it, or refuse the case). On a
converged tetragonal nickel state at 40 Ry the reduced `E(x) - E(z)` goes from
+8.30e-3 meV to -6.8e-6 meV at `conv_thr = 1e-10`, and +7.3e-6 meV at `1e-12`, a floor
that does not fall with `conv_thr` and is carried in `OPEN.md` Part XIX item 2; P117 has
the table.

`defumat/paw/onecenter.py:268` gates the small component on `nspin == 4` alone
(`small = paw.density_rel if nspin == 4 else None`), which is QE's gate
(`paw_onecenter.f90:166`, `with_small_so = upf(i%t)%has_so .AND. nspin_mag==4`); QE has no
`soc_scale`, so the port inherited a gate that never had to know about one, and nothing in
`defumat/paw/` reads `soc_scale`. The term adds `-2 (m_small . r) r` to the sphere's
magnetization (`:440`) and its chain rule to `ddd` (`:483`), so it ties the spin direction
to the real-space direction on the sphere. The functional stays consistent, since `ddd` is
its derivative, but the reduced dataset is **not** coupling-free, and P115's sentence that
the reduced dataset "is then an ordinary scalar-relativistic one ... nothing else has to
change downstream" is false for a fully-relativistic PAW dataset.

The check, `onecenter_species` on `Ni.rel-pbe-spn-kjpaw_psl.1.0.0.UPF` (`nh = 34`) with a
random non-spherical collinear `becsum`, moment 0.4 of the charge block, rotated as a
whole, `axis=None`, one core:

| `density_rel` | `E(x) - E(z)` | `E(y) - E(z)` | `E(-z) - E(z)` |
|---|---|---|---|
| present, as the code runs | +1.0150e-01 meV | +8.8572e-02 meV | 0 |
| removed (`eqx.tree_at`) | 0 | 0 | 0 |

So the control that is meant to have no direction dependence has 0.1 meV of it on one
sphere, on the scale of the anisotropies it is subtracted from. The `becsum` is random
rather than converged, so this sizes the mechanism and not the error on a real cell. It
reaches `ni-tetragonal-relaxed-mae-paw.in`'s `soc_scale = 0` leg with no error, and the
one-file PAW force-theorem route takes its SCF leg from the same functional
(`workflows/anisotropy.py:72-86`, `:509-512`). No test covers it: the PAW force-theorem
test uses the scalar `O.pz-kjpaw`. Whether the leg should drop `density_rel` or keep it
and document that PAW's control carries it is a decision about what `soc_scale = 0`
*means*, not a fix.

Beside it, `defumat/paw/gradient.py:202-206` says `add_small_mag` is "not reproduced" in
the one-centre XC. The code contradicts it: `onecenter.py:440` applies it, the GGA branch
receives the corrected density at `:489-490`, and `gradient.py:254` returns the vector
potential for `g`. The script is `scratchpad/paw_small_rotation.py` in the session that
ran it; it is twenty lines and is described fully above.

## Confirmed, and each one is a wrong number on some path

**`defumat/pseudo/spinorbit.py:633`, `becsum_transform` ignores the value of `soc_scale`
below 1.** **[closed 2026-09-25, P118: it blends; both identities fail at 0.5 on the old code, 3 and 24 per cent]** It branches on `soc_scale == 1.0` and otherwise returns `T^T(P)`, the
`soc_scale = 0` map, at any value, while `dvan_so` (`spinorbit.py:360-364`), `qq_so`
(`:428-431`) and `newd_so` (`driver.py:865`) all return
`reduced + soc_scale * (coupled - reduced)`. At an intermediate scale the density is then
not the adjoint of the Hamiltonian, which is the P115 defect again. Unreachable today:
intermediate values are refused at `spinorbit.py:288`, `builder.py:352` and `:958`. But
`OPEN.md` Part XIX item 2 proposes lifting that refusal to measure a blend, on the
statement that "the consistent blend of all four sandwiches is a variational functional
at every scale", and this code would not compute that blend. The adjoint of
`T(V) + s (F V F - T(V))` is `T^T(P) + s (full(B) - T^T(P))`, and that one line is the fix.
The check is `tests/unit/test_soc_scale.py`'s two identities with `0.5` added to the
parametrisation and the three refusals patched: both should fail at `rel = 1e-12` as the
code stands, by a few per cent on the P115 numbers.

**`defumat/xc/functional.py:773`, the GGA response kernel now keeps negative-density
points that `ph.x` zeroes.** **[measured, P119: the kernel ratio is 3.2e-5 and the epsilon is 1.5e-4 from `ph.x`; the kernel-only A/B is still to run]** The kernel here is one `jvp` of `v_of_rho`
(`response/efield.py:578-583`, and `phonon.py:620` uses the same one), so gating the
potential on `|rho|` moved the kernel with it: at an active negative point
(`rho + rho_core < -1e-6`, `sigma > 1e-10`) it is now the second derivative of the signed
energy. `ph.x` has all four kernel arrays zero there: `setup_dgc.f90:153` calls `dgcxc` on
the signed `rhoout`, whose gate is `r_in > 1e-30` (`qe_drivers_d_gga.f90:105`), and
`setup_dgc.f90:156-159` zeroes `rhoout` where it is negative before the `xc_gcx` that
builds `dvxc_s`. Before `7efbc3d` both codes dropped those points. The skeptic's
correction is the statement to keep: this code's kernel is the exact derivative of its own
potential, `ph.x`'s is not the derivative of `pw.x`'s at those points, so this is a
departure from the reference's convention rather than an internal inconsistency, and the
same commit moved the ground state *toward* `pw.x`, so the net change of epsilon against
`ph.x` has an unknown size and an unknown sign. Reachable on
`bismuthene-epsilon-us-soc.in` (PBE, spinor, no moment, a core charge), which has a
committed `ph.x` reference of 17.732384482 in-plane and whose total moved by 3.54e-5 Ry
when those points were included (P116), so the points exist. No committed GGA response
reference on a slab has been run. The sizing route is one Sternheimer solve at a fixed
`drho`: `<drho|(K_new - K_old) drho> / <drho|K drho>` with the old signed gate patched in
for the kernel call only; the full epsilon A/B is worth running only if that ratio is
above about 1e-6.

**`defumat/workflows/anisotropy.py:999`, `frozen_expectation` leaves out the `newd_so`
difference.** **[measured, P119: not zero, 1.26e-2 meV on the cubic cell and 1.79e-3 meV of
first-order anisotropy on tetragonal cobalt; the fix is `OPEN.md` Part XIX item 3]** For an ultrasoft dataset the coupled Hamiltonian minus the reduced one at a
frozen potential is `delta dvan_so + [F B F - T(B)] - eps delta qq_so`, and the function
evaluates the first and last terms only. The omission predates `a9eb343`, which changed
what the omitted term is (it was `F B F - B` before). The reviewer rated it low, and it is
not: the smoke cell behind the recorded **+/-0.000001 meV, direction-independent to
1.9e-6 meV** (the module docstring, `PLAN.md` near line 10800, and
`test_anisotropy.py:405-425`) is `Co.rel-pbe-nd-rrkjus`, ultrasoft, so that number is the
expectation of part of the operator. It is also reached as `Calculator` method
(`calculator.py:1918-1928`). Its direction-independence check sits on a simple cubic cell,
where symmetry forces the spread to zero, so that assertion cannot fire. The physics
expects the missing term to vanish at first order too, since the orbital moment of a
scalar-relativistic collinear state is quenched, so the headline probably survives; it is
unmeasured. The check is to add `sum w <psi|beta>(D1 - D0)<beta|psi>`, with
`D1 = _newd_noncollinear(..., 1.0)` and `D0` at `0.0` from the frozen potential, for x, y
and z on the smoke cell and on `co-tetragonal-relaxed-mae.in`, where symmetry does not
force the spread.

## What came back clean

**The Anderson fit.** With the flag off every numerical path is the old one: the metric is
`None` unless `run_scf` installs it, `_fits` stays empty, and the only unconditional change
is a `len__fits: 0` entry in the checkpoint metadata, which round-trips both ways. With the
flag on, `F` reproduces `rho_ddot`, `ns_ddot` and the `tau` term against
`scf_mod.f90:754-829` and `:937-999`, apart from the dense-set and `tau` differences listed
above.

**The spin trace.** The `becsum` transpose is the right one, checked index by index, and
`test_newd_is_the_derivative_of_the_augmentation_energy` would catch it swapped, since it
uses a complex Hermitian `B`. The map is applied to the zeroed `fcoef`, after `dvan_so` is
built from the full one, which is `init_us_1.f90:201-203`'s order. A fully-relativistic PAW
leg is now variational, the small component aside. Intermediate scales are refused on
every route. Forces, stress, the Sternheimer and Born paths, `int3`, the dipole and the
Berry-phase `q_ij(b)` all go through the reduced sandwiches; nothing rebuilds them at
scale 1 by accident. By reading, the ultracell's tiled null would pass.

**The Part III edits.** All three are identities in exact arithmetic and were measured
bit-identical; none is bit-identical by construction, and the measurement was taken on one
core only, so a multi-core GEMM sharding by the inner dimension is not excluded. The `M1`
jit was A/B'd only on the stored augmentation route, not the tabulated one. Nothing
geometry-, strain-, k-, q- or spiral-dependent is hoisted or cached, P73's remat on the
tabulated route is untouched, and the velocity operator still carries `eps dS/dk` and the
augmentation connection, with rule D4 left in the consumers.

**The `|rho|` gate.** `pw.x` signs `ex` and `ec` and nothing else
(`xc_wrapper_gga.f90:228-232`, and the libxc branch at `:263` and `:388`), so at an active
negative point this code's energy and `v1` are `pw.x`'s exactly and `v2` is its negative,
which is the whole pointwise difference. The assembled potential differs by the divergence
of `2 v2 grad rho` restricted to those points, which a G-space divergence spreads to their
neighbours, and eigenvalues, `force_cc`, `vtxc` and the stress see it at first order; the
analytic stress and force share the sign with the `jax.grad` route, so their agreement
cannot test it. `nspin = 2` and a noncollinear run with a moment still gate on the signed
density, as `gcx_spin` and `gcc_spin` do, and PAW passes `|rho|` with no sign, as
`paw_onecenter.f90:781` does. `abs` at zero is behind the `active` mask and the sign's
tangent is zero. The threshold compares the total with the core added, as `pw.x` does, and
`test_xc.py:246-272` fails on the old code.
